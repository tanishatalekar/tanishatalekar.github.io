About Me

I am a first year PhD student at the Australian National University, Mathematical Sciences Institute, supervised by Dr. Asilata Bapat. I am mainly interested in the combinatorics of certain objects that arise from categorical and geometric representation theory.

Research Interests

I am currently thinking about various combinatorial objects like convex orders on positive systems of roots, reduced expressions of the long element, Bridgeland stability conditions, pseudoline arrangements, subword complexes, and ppt (pointed pseudo-triangulation) complexes. The aim is to build a combinatorial dictionary to link all these objects.

In 2024, wrote a sage code with Dr. Asilata Bapat which computes the stable objects for a Bridgeland stability condition on a 2 Calabi-Yau category
